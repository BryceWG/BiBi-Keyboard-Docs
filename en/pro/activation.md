# Pro Activation

BiBi Keyboard Pro is distributed via **Google Play**. There are multiple ways to purchase/activate:

- Buy directly in Google Play
- Buy a promo/activation code via the Telegram group owner, then redeem it via an activation link or during purchase
- Learn more in OSS `Settings → About → Learn about Pro`, and purchase via WeChat/Alipay QR payment; then email required info to the author to receive an activation code

::: tip Note
"Activation code" here refers to a Google Play promotion redemption code.
:::

## Channels

### Option 1: Google Play

**Price**: $4.49 USD (one-time purchase; regional pricing may vary)

Steps:

1. Search "BiBi Keyboard Pro" / "说点啥 Pro" on Google Play
2. Purchase
3. Activated automatically after payment
4. Install the Pro app

::: tip Note
Pro package is `com.brycewg.asrkb.pro` and can coexist with OSS (`com.brycewg.asrkb`).
:::

**Price**: ¥32 CNY

Pros:

- ✅ no need to join the group

1. In OSS, open `Settings → About → Learn about Pro`
2. Save the Alipay/WeChat QR code
3. Pay and email the author with order screenshot to get an activation link/code

## FAQ

::: details How to activate after switching devices?

Google Play purchase is bound to your Google account. Sign in with the same Google account on the new device:

1. Install BiBi Keyboard Pro from Google Play
2. Sign in with the same Google account used for purchase
:::

## Refund policy

### Google Play purchase

- **Within 2 hours**: automatic refunds are supported

  1. Open Google Play
  2. Go to `Account → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history` (wording may vary)
  3. Select BiBi Keyboard Pro
  4. Tap `Request a refund`

- **After 2 hours**: contact Google Play support; policies vary by case

### Activation code purchase

- Generally not refundable; contact the group admin for special cases

::: warning Note
After a refund, the activation will be revoked and Pro features will no longer be available.
:::
