# OSS vs Pro Comparison

BiBi Keyboard provides two editions: OSS and Pro. This page compares feature differences.

## Positioning

- **OSS**: free, includes core speech recognition features for most daily usage
- **Pro**: one-time purchase ($4.49 / ¥32), adds advanced features for power users

## FAQ

### Can I get a refund after buying Pro?

- **Google Play**: automatic refund within 2 hours (policy may vary)
- **Activation codes**: generally not refundable

See [Activation - Refund policy](./activation#refund-policy).

### Is there a Pro trial?

No dedicated trial at the moment, but OSS already includes most core features, so you can try OSS first.

### Subscription or one-time purchase?

**One-time purchase**. It is bound to your Google account. No subscription.

### Can I use Pro on multiple devices?

- **Google Play purchase**: yes, with the same Google account

### Can OSS and Pro coexist?

Yes. Pro package is `com.brycewg.asrkb.pro`, so it can be installed alongside OSS (`com.brycewg.asrkb`).
